## Decisions
- **2026-10-15** **iPhone keeps one ONNX runtime** (user: the reload always happens, fix it). Why: each runtime reserves its WASM memory up front, and two of them plus the camera are what Safari kills. Bus camera on iPhone reads the sign inside the detector's runtime (256 MB, plain WASM, 1 thread), not a second worker. The reader on iPhone uses the plain WASM build at 512 MB, and the page reader is terminated before the voice starts; aiming again drops the voice first. The line canvas only grows. The service worker no longer calls `skipWaiting` or `clients.claim`, so a new worker cannot take the open page. Not yet watched on a real phone.
- **2026-10-15** **SOS = two-finger hold, calls the family** (user: make SOS faster; iPhone has no vibration, so the signal is a loud tone). Why two fingers: one-finger hold is already "talk". Why call, not SMS: the SMS app still needs a Send tap a blind rider can't find; one tap on a call is enough. Fires on lift (a real tap; Safari/Chrome only open the dialer from a gesture, a timer is too late). Voice SOS ("тусламж хэрэгтэй") stays: SMS menu with double tap, now without waiting for the server. Always-on voice listening rejected (Speechmatics credit, battery, iPhone stops the mic when locked, false triggers, privacy).
- **2026-10-15** **Chimege for the final speech text** (user: use Chimege for STT, but don't call it a lot: the credit is for tomorrow). Why: our tests on 77 Mongolian clips showed Speechmatics mishears the FIRST word almost every time ("Ерэн ес" -> "Ер нь эс"/"Ерөнхий", "тэг" -> "тухыг"), so phone numbers mostly failed; Chimege (Mongolian ASR) got both test numbers exactly ("ерэн ес арван нэг хорин хоёр гучин гурав", digit by digit with "тэг"). How: Speechmatics still streams (it says when the rider starts/stops, shows the words while they talk, and is the backup text); the phone keeps the recording (16 kHz, up to 15 s) and sends it ONCE to `/api/stt/chimege` (our server adds the token, `server/chimege.py`; key `CHIMEGE_API_KEY` in .env + Vercel env) as soon as the rider stops; Chimege's text wins, Speechmatics' stands if Chimege fails/slow (8 s). Paid 15₮ per call (<= 15 s): never for silence (only when words were heard or audio was clearly loud for 0.3 s), never retried, nothing ahead of time; short answers padded to Chimege's 50 KB minimum. Also for numbers: listening waits 1.8 s of quiet (people pause between pairs; 0.7 s cut them off) and stops as soon as 8 digits are complete; the parser reads digits, pairs, 4-digit groups (мянга/зуу), "тэг", misheard forms; a partial first try asks in two halves of 4 digits; the number is always read back. Chimege calls used while building: 4 (60₮).
- **2026-10-15** **Reader built on its own page `/read`** (user: "make this one in another page"; free; 90%+; efficient; deployable for free). The bus flow only gained one home option ("Бичиг унших") and a voice route ("ном унш", "энийг уншаад өг" open it; "дахин унш" still asks the bus assistant). Everything on the phone, offline after the first visit:
  - **Capture**: the bus sign reader's text detector on 960 px frames (480 px found only 19% of lines on a whole page; 960 px 99.9%) → ticks (higher = closer to ready), short directions, auto-capture after 2 ready frames (or 9 s with text in view), torch in the dark; full-res photo (≤3000 px). "Move closer" only below the line height that still read 99.3% (0.003 of the frame with a full-res photo, 0.0045 with only a video frame).
  - **OCR**: same detector at 1280 px + PaddleOCR `cyrillic_PP-OCRv5_mobile_rec` (Apache-2.0, 8 MB). Measured on 22 synthetic phone photos of real Mongolian text: books 99.6%, 3-column newspapers 98.9%, labels 99.5% of characters right; v5 detector worse on labels (77.9%: missed white text on a red band), so v4 kept; Tesseract not needed. Crops grow only halfway into the free space above/below (tight newspaper lines otherwise showed their neighbours: +1.2 points).
  - **Reading order**: XY-cut on deskewed lines with leaning gutters (tilted photos make columns lean: straight cuts read 3 columns in horizontal strips), cuts only between paragraph text (menus/price lists stay row by row), rows of the same column layout merged first.
  - **Orientation**: sideways pages (mostly vertical lines) turned 90°; upside-down caught when the first lines read poorly (<0.85) and much better turned (+0.15): costs nothing on normal pages.
  - **Confidence**: ≥0.8 read; 0.6-0.8 soft tone first; <0.6 "Тодорхойгүй мөр" (measured: below 0.8 only ~70-75% of characters right).
  - **Voice** (user: "never read Mongolian with the Russian voice"; "if the Meta TTS is bad use OronTTS"): Meta MMS-TTS mon only, never the phone's voice; a soft loading sound until it's ready; fixed reader sentences from pre-made OronTTS clips when they exist. OronTTS can't read arbitrary text for free (1.5 GB, ~8 s per sentence on an M4 GPU; no free GPU host), so it stays for pre-made clips. MMS: ONNX, weights packed 114 → 35 MB (int8 decoder+flow, fp16 rest: log-mel change 0.02-0.04 vs 0.5 between two runs of the model itself); its training alphabet wrote ш as щ and ө as ѳ (same ids), so they're mapped. WebGPU when the phone has it (measured 6.8 s of speech in 0.46 s) else WASM (in 4.1 s); spoken piece by piece (punctuation), the sentence being read first, the next two made meanwhile.   - **Offline**: `public/sw.js` (the app's one service worker; its reader part: scope `/` because the workers live under `/_next/static/`; it answers only `/read`, `/_next/static/*`, `/ort/*`; bus pages, `/api`, `/tts` untouched), files the page and workers used are kept after the first load; models in Cache Storage. - **2026-10-15** **The camera never confirmed the bus on iPhone** (user: "it cannot detect the bus").
